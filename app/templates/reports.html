Review workflow

  1. Pick a template and fill in only what the reviewer needs.
  2. Preview locally; every value is escaped and masked by default.
  3. Download the bundle and send the zip. The reviewer opens report.html; no login or Breadcrumb install is required.
  4. They can verify integrity with python -m app.cli verify-bundle review.zip or sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.

Redaction applies to the report, comments and evidence manifests. Custom rules are additive and can never turn off the built-in masking. Raw capture bodies stay in this local database.
